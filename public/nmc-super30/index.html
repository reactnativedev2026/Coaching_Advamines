Parents' Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which classes can enquire for NMC coaching?

Parents and students can enquire about Foundation programs for Classes 6–10, Class 11–12 JEE/NEET preparation, the Super 30 Elite Batch and long-term NEET repeaters.

Is Super 30 only for NEET students?

The Super 30 Elite Batch supports focused entrance preparation for NEET and IIT-JEE aspirants. Our academic team can explain the subject pathway suitable for your goals.

Do you offer long-term NEET preparation after Class 12?

Yes. NoMoreClass offers a long-term NEET repeater program for students who have completed Class 12 and want structured preparation for their next attempt.

Does NoMoreClass offer Foundation coaching?

Yes. Foundation and board-focused support is available for Classes 6–10, with emphasis on Mathematics, Science, analytical thinking and consistent practice.

Who teaches the classes?

NoMoreClass works with experienced senior faculty and IIT alumni. Teaching support and faculty arrangements vary by course and center; the academic team will share the applicable details.

When does the Super 30 Elite Batch begin?

The planned Super 30 intake is April 2027. Please enquire for updated schedules and availability for your preferred program.

How can I get fee details and batch timings?

Submit the admission enquiry form or call our academic team for the Hebbal or Yelahanka campus. A counsellor can explain the course options, latest batch timings and applicable fees.
