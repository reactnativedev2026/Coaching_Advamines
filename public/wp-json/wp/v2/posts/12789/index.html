<\/span>The Indian Institute of Science<\/span><\/span><\/h1>

In the 1890s, Jamsetji Nusserwanji Tata, a successful industrialist, decided to set up a world class university in India using his personal wealth. He strongly believed in the role of scientific research and higher education in social and economic transformation.<\/p>

        Tata\u2019s dream of establishing what eventually came to be known as the\u00a0Indian Institute of Science<\/a>\u00a0(IISc) became a reality with the support of\u00a0the\u00a0Mysore State, whose rulers also shared his commitment towards education and research. The Regent Queen Maharani\u00a0Kempananjammani\u00a0Vani\u00a0Vilasa\u00a0Sannidhana\u00a0\u2013 her son\u00a0Krishnaraja\u00a0Wadiyar was a minor then \u2013 provided 371 acres and 16\u00a0guntas\u00a0of land in Bangalore,\u00a0funds for\u00a0capital expenditure, and an annual contribution for Tata\u2019s ambitious project.\u00a0The remaining money to set up IISc came from the\u00a0colonial\u00a0government of India.<\/p>

        After overcoming several hurdles, including those resulting from Tata\u2019s\u00a0untimely\u00a0death\u00a0in the summer of 1904, IISc finally came into existence on 27\u00a0May\u00a01909\u00a0in Bangalore\u00a0following a vesting order and resolution passed by\u00a0the\u00a0government\u00a0of India\u00a0to\u00a0establish\u00a0the Institute.\u00a0Its\u00a0first Director was the English chemist Morris Travers.\u00a0Twenty-four students joined when the Institute opened its doors to students in 1911.<\/p>

        The Institute started with just two\u00a0academic\u00a0departments:\u00a0General and Applied Chemistry,\u00a0and Electrical Technology.\u00a0During those early years, urged by Sir M Visvesvaraya, the Dewan of Mysore who was\u00a0nominated\u00a0to IISc\u2019s\u00a0Council,\u00a0researchers carried out studies that\u00a0were of immediate importance to the country. This research even\u00a0led to the establishment of six factories in less than five years. The most successful of these were the soap and sandalwood oil factories\u00a0in Bangalore and Mysore.\u00a0The Institute\u00a0also\u00a0grew to include\u00a0departments such as those of Biochemistry and Physics. The latter\u00a0was set up under Sir CV Raman, a Nobel Laureate who\u00a0also became\u00a0the first Indian\u00a0Director of IISc\u00a0in 1933.<\/p>

        During World War II, IISc contributed towards the war effort by training\u00a0personnel, manufacturing military and industrial goods, and collaborating with\u00a0Hindustan Aircraft Limited to repair and maintain British and American war planes. This period saw\u00a0an\u00a0expansion of\u00a0research in engineering, and new departments such as those\u00a0of Aeronautical Engineering,\u00a0Metallurgy,\u00a0and\u00a0Mechanical Engineering\u00a0were added\u00a0in the 1940s.<\/p>

        In the 1960s,\u00a070s\u00a0and early 80s,\u00a0under\u00a0the\u00a0stewardship of Director Satish Dhawan (an\u00a0eminent\u00a0aerospace engineer who also led ISRO), the\u00a0Institute grew\u00a0further\u00a0to include a diverse range of research areas\u00a0from\u00a0materials\u00a0science,\u00a0computer\u00a0science and\u00a0automation,\u00a0and\u00a0molecular\u00a0biophysics, to\u00a0interdisciplinary work under the Centre for Theoretical Studies, which\u00a0eventually\u00a0led to the formation of other\u00a0centres\u00a0in\u00a0ecology, atmospheric and oceanic sciences, and more.\u00a0The social impact of advancements in science was also a key focus during this period, particularly\u00a0under the Cell for\u00a0Application of Science and Technology to Rural Areas (ASTRA), which continues today as the Centre for Sustainable Technologies.<\/p>

        Moving into the twenty-first century, IISc has\u00a0set\u00a0up an undergraduate\u00a0programme,\u00a0several new departments\u00a0and\u00a0centres\u00a0in the areas of brain research, nanoscience and engineering,\u00a0hypersonics\u00a0and more, strengthened ties with industry,\u00a0and incubated\u00a0several\u00a0start-ups.\u00a0It\u00a0has also\u00a0expanded to include a\u00a01500-acre campus at\u00a0Challakere\u00a0in\u00a0Chitradurga\u00a0district, Karnataka.<\/p>

        IISc counts among its\u00a0former\u00a0students and\u00a0faculty\u00a0several eminent scientists such as Homi J Bhabha, the founder of India\u2019s nuclear program, Vikram Sarabhai, the founder\u00a0of India\u2019s space\u00a0programme,\u00a0the meteorologist\u00a0Anna Mani,\u00a0the biochemist and\u00a0nutrition expert Kamala\u00a0Sohonie,\u00a0and solid state\u00a0and\u00a0materials scientist CNR Rao, to name just a few.\u00a0The Institute also played a\u00a0key\u00a0role in establishing other\u00a0academic\u00a0institutions\u00a0and\u00a0organisations.<\/span><\/p>

        IISc has welcomed a number of distinguished guests, from Mohandas K. Gandhi and members of the Mysore royal family to Queen Elizabeth and officials of the British Raj, heads of state such as Ho Chi Minh of Vietnam and Nikita Khrushchev of the USSR, Nobel Laureates such as James Watson and Brian Schmidt, academicians such as the Tanzanian minister and diplomat WK Chagula, and Indian Presidents and Prime Ministers\u00a0such as\u00a0Rajendra Prasad, Pratibha Patil, Jawaharlal Nehru,\u00a0HD\u00a0Deve Gowda,\u00a0Manmohan Singh,\u00a0and Narendra Modi.<\/p>\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t

        <\/span>From the Director <\/strong><\/span><\/h2>

        The Indian Institute of Science (IISc) was established in 1909 by a visionary partnership between the industrialist Jamsetji Nusserwanji Tata, the Mysore royal family and the Government of India.<\/p>

        Over the last 111 years, IISc has become India\u2019s premier institute for advanced scientific and technological research and education. Its mandate is \u201cto provide for advanced instruction and to conduct original investigations in all branches of knowledge as are likely to promote the material and industrial welfare of India.\u201d In keeping with this guiding principle, the Institute has strived to foster a balance between the pursuit of basic knowledge and applying its research for industrial and social benefit.<\/p>

        IISc\u2019s reputation and pre-eminence ensures that it attracts the best young faculty members trained in the best laboratories around the world. In 2018, IISc was selected as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) by the Government of India, and it consistently figures among the top Indian institutions in world university rankings.<\/p>

        IISc\u2019s research output is diverse, interdisciplinary and cuts across traditional boundaries. The Institute has over 42 academic departments and centres that come under six divisions. It also places equal emphasis on student learning, with about 4000 students pursuing several postgraduate and PhD programmes, as well as a dedicated four-year undergraduate programme aimed at providing research-oriented training for young students in the basic sciences.<\/p>

        IISc has a vibrant and diverse campus spread over 440 acres of greenery in the city of Bengaluru (formerly Bangalore), India\u2019s hub of high-tech companies (in aerospace, electronics, and information technology), educational and research institutions, and numerous start-ups. With the help of a recently-established office called DIGITS, we are now in the process of creating a best-in-class IT and networking system.\u00a0In recent times, IISc has also entered into collaborations with several technology giants to find solutions to problems in strategic areas. Many of its faculty members have established their own start-ups\u00a0to take their research directly to society.<\/p>

        During its centenary in 2009, IISc acquired a new campus in Challakere taluk, Chitradurga district, Karnataka. The flagship project of this 1,500-acre campus, a training programme for rural science and mathematics school and college teachers, has trained more than 11,000 teachers, and has been recognized as a Centre for Excellence by the Government of India.<\/p>

        In the coming years, IISc aims to position itself among the world\u2019s foremost academic institutions. We will focus on building our core research strengths in all frontiers of science and engineering, develop world-class teaching programmes, nurture translational research and encourage the incubation of successful start-ups. We will continue to empower researchers from diverse disciplines to work together to solve pressing challenges. We will also continue to carry out activities with direct social impact, such as training school teachers, disseminating sustainable rural technologies, and research in areas like climate change, healthcare, water management, and renewable energy. At the same time, we seek to embrace modern professional practices, and benchmark ourselves against international standards.<\/p>

        As we move forward to transform this vision into reality, we will create endless opportunities for students, innovators, teachers, researchers and more in the coming years. I welcome you to join us in this exciting journey.<\/p>

        The Indian Institute of Science <\/a>(IISc) offers a wide range of academic programs across various disciplines, catering to both postgraduate and undergraduate students. Here are some of the programs typically offered by IISc:<\/p>

        1. **Postgraduate Programs:**<\/p>

        – Master of Science (MSc): IISc offers MSc programs in various disciplines, including Biological Sciences, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, and more.<\/p>

        – Master of Technology (MTech): MTech programs are available in areas such as Aerospace Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Computer Science, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Materials Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and more.<\/p>

        – Master of Design (MDes): IISc provides a Master of Design program, typically in the area of Product Design and Engineering.<\/p>

        – Master of Management (M.Mgt.): IISc offers a two-year Master of Management program focusing on business and management in the technology domain.<\/p>

        2. **Research Programs:**<\/p>

        – Doctor of Philosophy (PhD): IISc is renowned for its PhD programs, which cover a wide array of scientific and engineering disciplines.<\/p>

        3. **Undergraduate Programs:**<\/p>

        – Bachelor of Science (Research) Program: IISc offers a unique four-year undergraduate program that focuses on research-oriented training in the basic sciences.<\/p>

        4. **Interdisciplinary Programs:**<\/p>

        – IISc encourages interdisciplinary research and often offers programs that bridge multiple fields, allowing students to engage in cross-disciplinary studies and research.<\/p>
