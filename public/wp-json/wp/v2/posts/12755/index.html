<\/span>Indian Institute of Technology Madras<\/strong><\/span><\/h1>

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) was founded in 1959. The institute was established through an Act of Parliament and was founded by the Government of India in collaboration with the Government of West Germany. While the institute itself was established through governmental initiatives, it doesn’t have a single individual founder in the traditional sense. Instead, it was a collective effort by the Indian government and international collaboration to establish a premier institute of technology in India.<\/p>
