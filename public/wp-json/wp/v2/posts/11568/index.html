              <\/span>Calculating JEE Main 2023 Percentile Score:<\/strong><\/span><\/h3>

              The percentile score in JEE Main 2023 is determined by comparing a candidate’s performance with that of other test-takers. To calculate the percentile score, the following formula is used:<\/p>

              Percentile Score = (No. of candidates with a raw score equal to or less than the candidate) \/ (Total No. of candidates who appeared in that session) x 100<\/strong><\/p>

              For example<\/strong>, if a candidate obtains a raw score of 200 out of 360, and there were 100,000 candidates in that session, with 80,000 scoring equal to or less than the candidate, then the percentile score would be 80%.<\/p>

              <\/span>Understanding Normalization Process:<\/strong><\/span><\/h4>

              The National Testing Agency (NTA) employs a unique normalization process to ensure fairness when comparing scores across different sets of question papers. This process accounts for variations in difficulty levels among the different sets, ensuring that no candidate is disadvantaged due to the complexity of their particular set.<\/p>

              The normalization process<\/strong> takes into account factors such as the number of candidates, the difficulty level of question sets, and the average performance of candidates in each set. By doing so, it levels the playing field for all candidates and ensures that their percentile scores accurately reflect their performance.<\/p>\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t
