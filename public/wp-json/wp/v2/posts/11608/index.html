          Admission Process<\/strong> To secure a seat in the IITs, students must successfully navigate the highly competitive Joint Entrance Examination (JEE). The JEE consists of two stages:<\/p>

          <\/span>JEE Main:<\/strong><\/span><\/h1>

          The preliminary stage of the examination that tests a student’s aptitude in mathematics, physics, and chemistry. The\u00a0JEE\u00a0<\/a>Main score is used for admission to various engineering colleges across India.<\/p>

          <\/span>JEE Advanced:<\/strong><\/span><\/h2>

          Only students who clear the JEE Main are eligible to appear for the JEE Advanced. This stage is more challenging and serves as the gateway to admission into the prestigious IITs.<\/p>
